Drag tasks into the order you want

On the deck, hold a tab by its title and drop it higher or lower. In All Tasks, use the handle on the right of a task. Either way, the other follows.

Lists start themselves

Begin a line with a dash, 1. or [ ] and a space, and it becomes a bullet, a numbered list or a checklist. Backspace straight after gives you back what you typed.
